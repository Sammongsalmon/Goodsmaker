GoodsMaker Privacy Policy

App: GoodsMaker · Package: com.sammongsalmon.goodsmaker

Effective: 9 September 2026

Summary GoodsMaker does not collect any personal data. There is no account and no sign-in, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Images you open and artwork you create are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

1. Data we collect

None. The app collects no names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts or device identifiers. There is no registration or login.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps a few things in local device storage only. Nothing is uploaded, and all of it is removed when you uninstall the app.

  • Display preferences (theme, text size) — browser local storage
  • Your in-progress artwork and cutline settings — on-device IndexedDB
  • Fonts you add yourself — on-device IndexedDB
  • Exported PNG / PDF / AI files — saved only when you tap save, to the location you choose

3. Sharing with third parties

None. No ad networks, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social login SDKs. The app makes no requests to external servers.

4. Internet use

The app declares Android's INTERNET permission because its interface is built with web technology and loads files bundled inside the app. Everything it reads — including the font list — ships inside the installation package. No external communication takes place.

5. Permissions

  • INTERNET — to load the app's own bundled interface files (no external traffic)
  • VIBRATE — short haptic feedback on some interactions

The app never requests camera, location, contacts, microphone or address book access.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children, and since it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

7. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Settings and artwork stored on your device can be cleared with the app's Reset function, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Security

All processing happens on your device and no data leaves it, so there is no transmission to intercept. Work saved on the device is sandboxed per-app by Android.

9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
